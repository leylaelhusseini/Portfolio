CodeBrave

I was accepted into CodeBrave, a Lebanese non-profit that teaches computer science and AI to underserved children and youth.

Since 2018 it has brought tech education to public schools, shelters, and community centers, so young people can build skills for the careers of the future.

Teaching & Mentoring

In Grade 9, I worked with students with different learning, communication, and behavioral needs.

I adapted activities and the way I communicated with each student, using play, conversation, and shared activities to build trust and help them feel more included. I also encouraged other students to look beyond differences and accept their classmates as they were.

The experience taught me patience, adaptability, and how much trust can change the way someone learns and communicates.
